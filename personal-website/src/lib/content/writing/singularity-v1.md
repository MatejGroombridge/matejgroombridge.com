## I

humanity is standing at the threshold of a new intelligence era.

I think we are within 10 to 15 years of AI models surpassing human intellectual capacity across the board and reaching a point of recursive self-improvement. the upsides are enormous, and so is the downside risk.

what I want to do here is sketch the current state of AI development and where I think the next decade goes.

## II

progress has accelerated sharply since ChatGPT. the frontier labs have all converged on the same near-term goal, which is automating the software development cycle.

code is the natural target. it is deterministic, there is an enormous amount of clean training data, and unlike prose it can be executed — so correctness is checkable without a human in the loop. that makes it ideal for reinforcement learning.

it is also where the commercial value is most obvious, and where the flywheel is tightest: the labs are building the tools they will use to build their next models.

METR's time-horizon benchmark shows this clearly. the first ChatGPT model managed tasks of around 36 seconds. recent frontier models are measured in hours, and the number has been doubling roughly every 7 months.

my bet is that this curve continues until AI systems can run the research process themselves.
