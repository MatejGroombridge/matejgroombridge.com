For years I told myself I would start writing "soon." Soon never arrived on its own, so I am setting it down here as a quiet promise to myself.

## The honest reason

I have spent a long time reading other people's thinking and very little time recording my own. The ideas I think I understand turn out to be slippery the moment I try to put them into sentences. Writing is the cheapest way I know to find out what I actually believe.

## The selfish reason

I want a place that is mine. Not a feed, not a thread, not a chat log that disappears into a search bar. A handful of pages I can come back to in five years and recognise.

## The practical reason

The next decade of my career will be shaped by how clearly I can think on paper. I would rather practise that here, on a small website nobody is forced to read, than only in the documents that matter most.

So: I am writing. Not because I have a lot to say, but because the only way to find out is to start.
